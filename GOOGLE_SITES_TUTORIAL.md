## Step 4: Add & Showcase MS Excel, Google Sheets, & Word Skills

### How to Embed Live Google Sheets (Interactive Spreadsheets):
1. Open **[drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)** and upload your Excel file or create a new Google Sheet (e.g., `Abhishek_Portfolio_Data.xlsx`).
2. On Google Sites, go to the **Insert** panel on the right.
3. Scroll down to **Google Docs Editors** section and click **Sheets**.
4. Select your spreadsheet file and click **Insert**.
5. *Result*: Visitors will see your live interactive spreadsheet right inside your Google Site!

### How to Embed Your Resume (Google Docs / PDF):
1. Upload `Page_Resume.docx` to Google Drive.
2. In Google Sites, click **Insert > Drive** (or **Docs**).
3. Select your resume file.
4. Set permission in Google Drive to "Anyone with the link can view".
5. Visitors can now scroll through your resume or download it directly from your website!

## Step 6: Publish Your Website 24/7 for Free!

1. In the top right corner of Google Sites, click the blue **Publish** button.
2. Under **Web Address**, type your desired sub-domain name:
   > `abhishek-parmar-portfolio`
3. Your live URL will be:
   > `https://sites.google.com/view/abhishek-parmar-portfolio`
4. Click **Publish**!
